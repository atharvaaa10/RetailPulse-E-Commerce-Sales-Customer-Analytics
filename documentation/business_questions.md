# RetailPulse — Strategic Business Questions

This document outlines the core commercial, operational, and customer questions that this analytics project aims to answer.

---

## 1. Sales Performance

* What is the total gross and net revenue generated across the entire business?
* What is the monthly and quarterly sales trend, and is there visible seasonality?
* Which product categories generate the highest total revenue?
* Which specific products are the top 10 revenue drivers?
* What is the overall Average Order Value (AOV), and how does it fluctuate over time?
* What is the distribution of order values across transaction volume?
* How does sales volume compare across different sales channels (e.g., Web vs. Mobile App)?
* What is the overall impact of promotional discounts on gross sales vs. realized net revenue?
* What proportion of total revenue is accompanied by promotional coupon codes?

---

## 2. Customer Analytics & Demographics

* How many unique customers exist in the customer database, and how many are active purchasers?
* Which top 5% of customers generate the highest lifetime revenue (Pareto 80/20 rule)?
* What percentage of monthly orders come from first-time buyers vs. returning customers?
* Which customer tiers/segments (e.g., VIP, Regular, New) generate the highest revenue per customer?
* What is the age and gender distribution across customer segments?
* Which geographic states and cities have the highest customer concentration and sales density?
* What is the average time elapsed between registration date and a customer's first purchase?
* What is the customer repeat purchase rate and average order frequency per customer?
* Which customer acquisition channels yield the highest long-term Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)?

---

## 3. Product Catalog & Profitability

* Which individual products sell the highest volume of units?
* What is the gross profit margin by product SKU and product category `((Listed_Price - Cost_Price) / Listed_Price)`?
* Which categories generate high sales volume but deliver low profit margins?
* Are there products or categories experiencing negative profit margins after discounts are factored in?
* How does product rating correlate with product price point and sales volume?
* Which suppliers supply the most revenue-generating products, and what are their respective return rates?
* What is the Pareto distribution of products (do top 20% of SKUs drive 80% of sales)?
* Which product subcategories have the highest discount elasticity?

---

## 4. Operations, Logistics & Fulfillment

* What is the overall average delivery duration from order placement to customer delivery?
* Which geographic regions or states experience the highest average delivery transit times and delays?
* Is there a statistically measurable relationship between longer delivery times and lower customer review ratings?
* What percentage of total orders end up cancelled or returned?
* Which product categories and brands suffer from the highest return rates?
* How does shipping cost charged to the customer correlate with order value and cart abandonment/order status?
* What is the correlation between delivery delay and customer return probability?
* Which fulfillment channels or suppliers have the highest operational fulfillment failure rates?

---

## 5. Payments & Checkout Experience

* Which payment methods (Credit Card, Debit Card, UPI, COD, Net Banking) are most preferred by order volume and total revenue?
* Does payment method preference significantly vary across customer segments or age brackets?
* Does payment method choice differ between high-ticket orders versus low-ticket purchases?
* Is Cash on Delivery (COD) associated with higher order cancellation or return rates compared to prepaid methods?
* What is the average order discount percentage across payment methods?

---

## 6. Advanced Analytical & Cross-Functional Questions

* **Customer Cohort Retention**: What percentage of customers acquired in each cohort month continue to make purchases in subsequent months?
* **RFM Segmentation**: When customers are grouped into Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) tiers, what percentage fall into "Champions", "Loyal Customers", "At Risk", and "Lost"?
* **Basket Analysis & Category Affinity**: What categories or products are frequently purchased together or by the same customer profiles?
* **Discount Cannibalization**: Are steep discounts genuinely driving incremental volume or simply eroding margins for products that sell well at list price?
* **Marketing Channel ROI & Quality**: Which acquisition channels bring customers who have lower return rates and higher average order ratings?
* **Seasonal Demand Shifting**: How do category-level sales mixes shift during festive or peak discount months compared to off-peak periods?
* **Geographic Logistics Optimization**: Which state-level clusters exhibit high sales volume but severe delivery lag, indicating a need for localized fulfillment centers?
* **Customer Lifetime Value by Acquisition Source**: What is the 12-month cumulative revenue progression of customers acquired via Paid Social versus Organic Search?
